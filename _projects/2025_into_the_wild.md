<p>
This project presents the experimental framework and findings from our paper <i>"Into the Wild: Real-World Testing for ML-Based ABR"</i> (PACMI@SOSP 2025). It focuses on robust, repeatable evaluation of ML-driven Adaptive Bitrate (ABR) algorithms outside synthetic testbeds, capturing real network dynamics and failure modes.
</p>

<h5>Overview</h5>
<p>
We outline the methodology for collecting real-world traces, orchestrating controlled deployments, and measuring QoE metrics under diverse network conditions. The page will be updated with artifacts (configs, scripts, logs) to help others reproduce and extend the results.
</p>
